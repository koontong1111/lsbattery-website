Step-by-step

Battery replacement guide

A simple 6-step process most drivers can complete in under 15 minutes — no mechanic needed.

⚠️ Safety first: Always turn off your engine and remove the key before starting. Work in a ventilated area away from sparks or flames.
1
Collect your battery from the locker
Select your locker, pay via the app or terminal, and retrieve your new battery. Check that the model matches your vehicle spec (printed on the locker door and your car manual).
✓ Keep your receipt — it activates your 12-month warranty
2
Open the bonnet and locate the battery
The battery is usually a rectangular black box with two cable terminals (red = positive +, black = negative −). On some models it may be under a plastic cover or in the boot.
3
Disconnect the old battery — negative first
Using a 10mm spanner, loosen and remove the black (−) cable first, then the red (+) cable. This prevents accidental short circuits. Tuck the cables away so they do not touch the terminals.
⚡ Never let the two cables touch each other
4
Remove the old battery and place it in the locker
Unbolt the hold-down clamp at the base and lift the battery straight out (it is heavy — ~15 kg). Return it to the same locker slot you collected from. The locker will lock automatically once it detects the returned unit.
5
Install the new battery — positive first
Lower the new battery into the tray, secure the hold-down clamp, then connect the red (+) cable first, followed by the black (−) cable. Tighten both terminals firmly — loose connections cause starting issues.
✓
Start your engine
Turn the key or press start. Your car should fire up immediately. Some vehicles may display a low-battery warning or reset their clock — this is normal and clears after a short drive.
✓ You are done — back on the road in under 15 minutes
